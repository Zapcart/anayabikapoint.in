EVERYTHING YOU NEED TO KNOW

Frequently Asked
Questions.

Still have a question? Ask on WhatsApp ↗
What products does Anaya Bike Point sell?+

We sell exclusively complete exterior body kits and individual body panels for scooters and motorcycles (including front aprons, side body panels, headlight cowls, visors, and mudguards). We do NOT sell riding gear, helmets, gloves, engine oil, chains, brake pads, spark plugs or mechanical spare parts.

Which vehicle models do you have body kits for?+

We specialize in Suzuki Burgman Street (old and new models), Suzuki Access 125 (old and new models), TVS Ntorq 125, Honda Activa (generations 3G, 4G, 5G, 6G), Honda CB Shine, Honda Unicorn, and other commuter bikes and scooters. Message us on WhatsApp to verify panel availability for your exact model.

What is included in a complete body kit?+

Depending on the vehicle, a complete kit typically includes the front apron (shield), handlebar headlight cowl, left and right side body panels, tail cowls, and front mudguard. We confirm the exact panel breakdown for your specific vehicle on WhatsApp before you decide to buy.

Can I buy just a single panel instead of a full kit?+

Yes. If you only need a front apron, a single left or right side panel, or a headlight cowl after minor damage, you can purchase individual panels separately.

How do I confirm the colour and fitment before buying?+

Send a photo of your vehicle and the damaged panel on WhatsApp to +91 9324396437 along with your vehicle model and year. Our team will verify the colour shade and mounting bracket compatibility.

Do you offer bike servicing or mechanical repairs?+

No, we are purely a body kit and exterior body panel specialist store. We do not provide mechanical servicing or repair appointments, but we can advise you on panel fitment.
