Still have a question? Ask us ↗
How do I place an order?+

Add items to your enquiry bag, enter your bike details and choose “Send enquiry on WhatsApp”. Our team confirms price, compatibility and availability before you decide to buy.

Will this part fit my bike?+

Compatibility depends on the exact model, variant and year. Use “Check fit” or send your bike details and a photo of the existing part to our team. Catalogue illustrations are representative.

Do you offer delivery or store collection?+

You can enquire about collection at our Kurla West shop. Ask our team about delivery availability, any charges and the expected timeline for your location before confirming an order.

Can I book a repair or service online?+

Yes. Use the service request form to send your preferred date and bike details on WhatsApp. A request is confirmed only after the shop agrees to the appointment.

What are your opening hours?+

We're open from 10:00 AM to 9:00 PM, Saturday through Thursday. Friday is our weekly off. Please call ahead on public holidays or before making a special trip.

What about warranty, returns and payment?+

Ask our team about the specific item's warranty, return eligibility and available payment methods before purchase. These depend on the product and will be confirmed with your quote.
